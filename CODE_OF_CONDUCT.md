# Code of conduct

The FynePDF open-source projects follow the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be respectful and patient, assume good intent, and focus on the work. Harassment, insults, personal attacks and discrimination aren't accepted, in issues, pull requests or anywhere else the projects are discussed.

## Reporting

If someone's behaviour makes you uncomfortable, email [support@fynepdf.com](mailto:support@fynepdf.com) with "Conduct" in the subject. Reports are kept confidential. The maintainers will review each report and act on it, which can range from a warning to a ban from the projects.
